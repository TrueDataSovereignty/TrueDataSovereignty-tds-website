The problem

Staff paste company data into AI.

Names, ID numbers, case notes: pasted in full into a service you do not run. It reads the text, and it may store it.

Your contract does not override it.

A US provider can be ordered to hand over data it controls, wherever that data is stored. A contract does not change that law. A data centre in the EU does not change who operates it.

18 U.S.C. § 2713 (the US CLOUD Act). EU location alone may not end US legal reach. Our reading, not legal advice.

The gate

A gate on your own machine, for every AI request.

The Layer reads each request before any model does. Then it decides what may leave. Three threads lead out of it.

Mask

Names leave as placeholders.

Names and numbers are hidden before the request goes to a frontier model. They are put back in the answer, on your side.

Generalise

Details soften, like light through fog.

Telling details are blurred. In our example, Sorsele leaves as northern Sweden.

Keep local

The thread curls back and seals.

The most sensitive work goes to a local model on your own machine. Nothing is sent out.

Masking protects who is in the text, not what it says: on the first two routes the model provider still reads the content. The local model is selected, not yet installed; the third route has not run live yet.

The record

  

    

      
Every request leaves a ring.

      
A tree keeps a ring for every year. The Layer keeps a record for every request: which route, which rule, what kind of data it found, which model. No text is in it.

      
Each record carries the fingerprint of the one before it. Take one out, and it shows.

      

      

    

    
  

  
Synthetic example. The five rings are the five real records from our demo run (router a822605). The fingerprint is a hash chain. A one-file evidence report, with no text in it, checks that chain for your auditor.

Why us

  
Many tools mask. The Layer first decides whether a request may leave at all.

  

    
A decision before the mask.
Three routes for every request, and one of them is local only. If masking is all you need, free tools do that.

    
Swedish IDs, by checksum.
Personal identity numbers and organisation numbers are found by their checksums. Other Nordic IDs are on the roadmap, not built.

    
A record for every request.
Model, provider, route and policy version, in a chain you can check. Decisions and counts, no text.

  

  
A prototype. The Layer does not write your text or the placeholder map to disk: they live in memory and are gone when it stops. (The operating system may page memory to disk.) No ISO 27001 or SOC 2 yet.
