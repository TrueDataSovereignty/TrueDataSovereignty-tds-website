What you write should stay yours to decide.

Every day, people hand their words to AI services they do not run. Names, cases, plans. A contract says the data is safe. A contract does not decide what happens to it.

We think that decision belongs to you. To a company first, because that is where we start. In time, to anyone: a person, a town, a country.

That is what sovereignty means to us. It is a kind of freedom.

The problem

Your contract does not override it.

Staff paste company data into AI. The service reads the text, and it may store it. A US provider can be ordered to hand over data it controls, wherever that data is stored. A data centre in the EU does not change who operates it.

18 U.S.C. § 2713 (the US CLOUD Act). EU location alone may not end US legal reach. Our reading, not legal advice.

The Layer

A gate on your own machine, for every AI request.

It reads each request before any model does. Then it decides what may leave.

Mask

The names and numbers it finds leave as placeholders. They are put back in the answer, on your side.

Generalise

Telling details leave blurred. Sorsele becomes northern Sweden.

Keep local

The most sensitive work goes to a local model on your own machine or your own server. Nothing is sent to a model provider.

Masking protects who is in the text, not what it says: on the first two routes the model provider still reads the content. The local model is selected, not yet installed; the third route has not run live yet.

The record

        
Every request leaves a ring.

        
A tree keeps a ring for every year. The Layer keeps a record for every request, with no text in it. Each record carries the fingerprint of the one before. Take one out or change it, and the check shows it. To catch the newest records being cut off, keep a copy of the latest fingerprint somewhere else.

        

        

      

      
    

    
Synthetic example. The five rings are the five real records from our demo run (router a822605). The fingerprint is a hash chain. A one-file evidence report, with no text in it, checks that chain for your auditor.

Until that gap closes, work on your confidential list stays local.

  
95% bounds are Clopper–Pearson; one-sided for V11-4. Each number is a row in our numbers ledger, with its commit, data hash and re-run command.
