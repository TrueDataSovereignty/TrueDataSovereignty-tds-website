The ground-line logo: vector files

Draft, not published. All files are written by make_logo.py, and the drawing is the same every time it runs. Nothing in web/brand/final is replaced.

The logo

Grown once by a seeded program, then fixed.

tds-tree-colour.svg

The logo on white or light paper. The tree in marine ink, the line in gold.

tds-tree-colour-on-marine.svg

The logo on marine blue. The tree in ivory, the line in gold.

tds-tree-one-ink.svg

One ink, midnight marine (#101A3D), line included. The standard dark version.

tds-tree-one-ink-reversed.svg

One ink, ivory on midnight marine, line included.

tds-tree-production-true-black.svg

Production fallback only: true black (#000000), one ink. For engraving, stamps and single-colour print where marine cannot be matched. Not for screens.

tds-tree-gradient.svg

Earlier look, kept for reference: roots fading to gold.

The small logo

Drawn by hand, stroke by stroke. This is the logo wherever it is small: header, footer, browser tab, app and extension icons. PNG copies (16, 32, 48, 128, 512 px, transparent) are in png/.

Full dark: one ink, midnight marine, line included.

tds-tree-small-black.svg

Full black (#000000), line included.

tds-tree-small-one-ink-reversed.svg

Full white, line included, shown on marine.
