Ideas we have not explored

Spore print

A mushroom cap left on paper leaves a unique radial print. Each customer, or each report, could get its own print made from its record: a seal nobody else has.

Lichen map

Lichen grows slowly outward on stone in pale rings and patches. A quiet, very Nordic texture for backgrounds, far from the usual tech look.
