The root fan, simplified, with the layer ring

Draft, not published. Number 2 from the last sheet, forged down: about 30 thread ends instead of several hundred, and no line thinner than a pen stroke, so it can be printed, stamped, embroidered or cut. A gold ring now sits on the stem. Mushrooms really have one there; it is called the ring. Ours is The Layer.

1 · The ring on the stem

One stem, one gold ring, one fan of roots. Everything below is joined to everything above through that ring. Marine trunks, gold ends.

96, 48 and 24 px, then one ink only

2 · Through the layer

Your idea taken all the way. Threads gather from above, pass through the one gold ring, and spread again below. Marine goes in, gold comes out. Nothing gets from one side to the other without passing the ring.

96, 48 and 24 px, then one ink only
