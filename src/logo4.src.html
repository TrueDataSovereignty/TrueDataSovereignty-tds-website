A solid gold disc that the trunk passes through, like a floor between two storeys. The most literal "layer": a surface, not a loop.

D · The diamond

One flat diamond plate, the exact shape of a layer in today's mark, pierced by the trunk. The strongest link between the old logo and the new.

E · The ground line

One straight gold line across the whole mark. Above it, what you see; below it, the network. The layer becomes the ground itself, which is also how the website is cut.
