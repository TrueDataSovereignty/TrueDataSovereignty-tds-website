TDS in the letters: five logo drafts

Draft, not published. Each shows the letters TDS with a mycelium link in them, on the light page and on marine blue, then small. These are sketches to choose a direction from. The chosen one gets drawn properly, with the letters as outlines.

1 · Thread

Lora letters with one gold thread running through all three, like a hypha passing through soil. Keeps today's typeface. Calm and bookish.

120 px and 60 px wide

2 · One hypha

The three letters are drawn as one line. Gold threads join T to D and D to S, with a small node at each joint. The most "linked" of the five, and the most modern. It would replace Lora in the logo.

120 px and 60 px wide

3 · Rooted

The letters stand on the ground. Below it, gold roots join them into one network. It tells the site's story in one picture: what you see above, and what connects it below.

120 px and 60 px wide

4 · Rings

The D holds gold growth rings, the record, and a thread links the letters through it. It carries the gold ring over from today's mark, so the change is gentler.

120 px and 60 px wide

5 · Seal

A gold ring with TDS inside and threads growing from its base. Made for small squares: the app icon, the extension button, a stamp on the evidence report. It can sit beside any of the other four.

64 px and 32 px

Today's mark, to compare

The stacked layers with the gold ring, from web/brand/final. Unchanged.

How to read this: drafts for direction only. The serif letters are live Lora text, so their exact shapes and the thread positions will be tuned once a direction is chosen. Nothing here replaces the files in web/brand/final until you say so.
