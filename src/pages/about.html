About

What you write should stay yours to decide.

True Data Sovereignty builds The Layer: a gate between your people and the AI services they use. It decides, request by request, what may leave.

The vision

Sovereignty is a kind of freedom.

Every day, people hand their words to AI services they do not run. Names, cases, plans. A contract says the data is safe. A contract does not decide what happens to it.

We think that decision belongs to you. To a company first, because that is where we start. In time, to anyone: a person, a town, a country.

That is what sovereignty means to us.
