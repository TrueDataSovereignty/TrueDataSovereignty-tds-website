The pilot

What you get, and what it takes.

You get

  • TDS on your machine, set to the local route only
  • A local dashboard that shows each routing decision
  • An evidence report for your CISO, built from the log only, with no text in it
  • A draft impact assessment (DPIA) for the pilot, our security answers and draft pilot terms
  • A delete-everything step, with a receipt

It takes

  • One workflow, and 20–50 made-up prompts
  • One machine you control that can run a local AI model
  • A few hours from your IT team. We have no access to your machine
  • Made-up text first. After that, only text you could publish
  • Blocking the machine's outbound traffic after install, and checking that the pilot still works

Why this way

What Swedish guidance says.

  • The national guidance on generative AI says that a tool run fully i egen regi (under your own control) means no disclosure has taken place. DIGG and IMY
  • eSam asks authorities to list the US cloud services they use, update their risk analyses and continuity plans, and plan for the EU–US adequacy decision falling. eSam
  • Before a contract is signed, the system should be test-run with synthetic data. Länsstyrelserna

Read at the source by our compliance team. Our reading, not legal advice.

Honest limits

What the pilot does not prove.

  • A prototype, not certified, with no external audit.
  • We have tested TDS itself: in its offline pilot mode it made no outbound connection attempts on our test machine. The local model's runner (Ollama) is a separate program we have not measured yet. That is why we ask you to block outbound traffic.
  • The local model is selected, not yet installed.
  • The other two routes stay off in the pilot. They open only by a written change, after a legal opinion.
  • The router can misjudge how sensitive a text is. In a pilot that sends nothing out, that does no harm, and it is one of the things the pilot measures.
  • It supports your own assessment under OSL, GDPR and NIS2; it does not make that assessment for you.
  • Nothing is signed before our company is registered.
