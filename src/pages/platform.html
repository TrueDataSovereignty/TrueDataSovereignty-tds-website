TDS gateway · for teams

One gate for every AI request.

TDS sits between your people and the AI models they use. For each request it decides what may leave, masks the identities it finds, keeps the key to put them back on the machine that runs TDS, and writes a record you can check.

See the three routes, with examples

What you get

Built for the people who must answer for the data.

A drop-in API

OpenAI- and Anthropic-compatible endpoints, so existing tools can point at TDS. Streaming and tool use work.

Your rules, per client and per task

Each client has its own token and its own profile. A missing or unknown setting makes a request stricter, never looser.

A record of every decision

One line per request: the route, the rule, counts and where the text went. No text from the request. Each line is chained to the one before, and the chain head can be anchored outside the log.

A local dashboard and an evidence report

See each decision, and give your auditor a self-contained report built from the log only. It says plainly what it cannot show.

When something goes wrong, it stays.

If TDS cannot finish its check (any error or timeout), the text stays on your machine. If the AI service itself is down, the request is refused, never sent elsewhere.

What it does not do yet

The limits, stated plainly.

  • A prototype, tested on synthetic data only. Real texts will do worse.
  • Masking protects who is in a text, not what it says: on the first two routes the model provider still reads the business content.
  • Detection is not perfect. Anything it misses is sent as written, including secrets said in other words.
  • The first two routes go to Anthropic in the US today. Zero-data-retention terms are not yet agreed. OpenAI and Gemini backends are built, with your own key, and are not switched on.
  • In a conversation, joining several messages can reveal more than each one alone. A fix is built and tested; it is off by default and on for pilots, and its figures are being re-checked.
  • The local model is selected, not yet installed.
  • Windows 11, one machine. No hardware enclave.
  • No ISO 27001 or SOC 2 certification. ISO 27001 is planned.
  • A privacy router, not a decision system. It supports your own assessment; it does not replace it.
